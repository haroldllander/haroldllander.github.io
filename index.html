About Me

I'm Harold Llander, a web developer and technical support specialist based in Quezon City, Philippines. I've spent over 9 years helping businesses keep their digital presence running smoothly — from resolving complex DNS issues to building full client websites from scratch.

I've worked with web hosting companies including Melbourne IT, HostPapa, and Bluehost/Newfold Digital, giving me deep hands-on experience with cPanel, WHM, DNS, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, SSL, and email troubleshooting.
